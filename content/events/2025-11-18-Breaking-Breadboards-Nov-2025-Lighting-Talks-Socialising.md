The Breaking Breadboards team are excited to announce our, Nov 18 Event at Freedom Works in Brighton. A lighting talks and socialising event as we head into the holiday season. This is the perfect opportunity for tech hardware enthusiasts in Brighton and beyond to connect and share their passion for all things tinkering!

This is the perfect opportunity to share your Raspberry PI, Arduino, microcontroller and robotics project. This can be shared by placing your project on our project bench and chatting about it informally. Or by giving a 5-minute lighting talk about the project. If you would like to do a lighting talk, please email jon@jondurrant before the event so we can get the order sorted.

Our current talks are as follows:

🚥 Mains Powered Traffic Light Timer - Mike Brewer  
📺 HMIs: Drag and drop UI design for microcontrollers - Nikhil Dabas  
💡 The Magic of Blinking Lights - Daz

Projects can be of any complexity, from beginner to product design. They can also be in any phase of development from concept to completion. All have value and will captivate us all. Don't be shy, share your project.

Find upcoming Breaking Breadboards events:  https://www.meetup.com/breaking-breadboards/events/
