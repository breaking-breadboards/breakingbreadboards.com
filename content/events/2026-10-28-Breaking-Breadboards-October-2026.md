Our **October 28th Event at Freedom Works in Brighton** was the perfect opportunity for tech hardware enthusiasts in Brighton and beyond to connect, and share their passion for all things tinkering!

Whether you’re into Raspberry Pi, Arduino, modifying Game Boys, building robots, or any other hardware-based projects, our events are for you. From seasoned professionals to complete beginners, everyone is welcome.

The event was **hybrid**, so if you couldn’t make it to Brighton, you can catch up on the recording on YouTube.
