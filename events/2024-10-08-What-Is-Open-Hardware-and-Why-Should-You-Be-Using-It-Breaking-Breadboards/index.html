What Is Open Hardware and Why Should You Be Using It? | Breaking Breadboards

A brief overview on what Open Hardware is, a couple of landmark community projects that show its power, and why and how it's being used at the University of Sussex to promote better research and education locally and globally. In the end we hope to have a lively conversation on why more people should be using it and how to get started!
