Breaking Breadboards - Launch Event - Show and Tell

15 May 2024

Breaking Breadboards is Brighton’s new space to meet those that tinker where hardware meets software. In this launch event we share some of our communities projects and get inspired by technology. Join this Show and Tell. Find out more about us on breakingbreadboards.com/

⭐ JOIN OUR COMMUNITY ⭐

We work hand-in-hand with Brighton's tech community to provide individuals with free opportunities for networking, training, up-skilling and development. No matter your experience level, there’s a place for you at Silicon Brighton, so join us today!
👉 https://siliconbrighton.com/
